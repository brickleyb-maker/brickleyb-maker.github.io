Report a problem

The fastest way: in PitchClip, open Settings, then Help, privacy and feedback, and send feedback. It fills in your app version, iPhone model and iOS version for you.

If something went wrong during a game, Settings also has a bug report you can share by email or AirDrop. It contains game details and recent logs, never video. It goes only to whoever you choose.

Getting started

  • Clip the phone to the backstop fence behind home plate, facing the pitcher.
  • Tap Record game. Every pitch saves as its own clip.
  • Set up the lineup by typing it, or by reading a screenshot or a photo of the lineup card.

Pitch counts

PitchClip counts pitches to help you follow Pitch Smart guidelines. Check the count yourself during the game, and follow your league's rules and your coach. PitchClip is a helper, not an official scorer.
