PitchClip Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

PitchClip does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you record and type stays on your iPhone. The developer receives nothing from the app unless you choose to send feedback or a bug report.

What stays on this phone

PitchClip keeps what it makes on this iPhone: the video of each pitch with game sound; each clip's details (game and opponent names, jersey numbers, any player names you type, pitch speeds and tags); lineups and your team list (My team); pitch counts and rest dates; the player photo you pick; your settings; the names of nearby phones in Live view; and a diagnostic log.

Clip videos, clip details and the diagnostic log are in PitchClip's folder, which you can also open in the Files app (On My iPhone, PitchClip). The log lists game and opponent names, jersey numbers and the names of nearby phones, never video. The pitch list, Recently deleted clips and the player photo are in PitchClip's private storage, which the Files app does not show. Exported clips and thumbnails sit in temporary storage that iOS can clear.

The app never sends any of this to the developer or to a server.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, iOS includes PitchClip's data in this iPhone's backup like any other app, including clip videos and the names in them. The player photo and temporary files are left out of backups. Backups are kept by Apple or on your computer, under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot see them.

To leave PitchClip out of iCloud Backup, turn it off in the iCloud Backup settings for this iPhone (Settings app, your name, iCloud).

Lineup screenshots

To fill in a lineup, PitchClip can read a GameChanger screenshot: one you pick, or the newest screenshot in your photo library if you allow PitchClip to see your photos. The text is read on this iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. The screenshot is not copied or sent anywhere. Only the jersey numbers and names you keep are saved.

Live view

Live view lets other phones running PitchClip near you follow the game. It is on by default. While "Share this phone's game with nearby phones" is on (Settings, Live view), a nearby PitchClip phone can connect and see this phone's name, the game name, the inning, pitch counts, the lineup with any names you typed, speeds, tags and clips. A clip that a watching phone opens is copied to that phone and stays there.

Who can watch is "Open to anyone with PitchClip" unless you choose "Ask me for each phone", which asks you to allow each new phone. "Let other phones control the game", also on by default, lets connected phones change things like the inning, the batter or the pitch count.

The connection is encrypted and goes directly between the phones over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, never through the internet or a server. Turn sharing off to keep a game private.

Sharing and saving

A clip, highlight video or speed card leaves PitchClip only when you share it or save it to Photos. After that it is handled by the app or person you sent it to, under their privacy terms. Move to Photos saves the original to your photo library and then removes the video from PitchClip. The pitch details and a still frame stay in PitchClip.

Feedback and bug reports

Send feedback (Settings, Help, privacy and feedback) opens an email to the developer in your mail app. It includes your message, the app version, phone model, iOS version and the diagnostic log as an attachment. You can read or remove anything before sending. The developer also sees your email address, because the email comes from you.

Send a bug report (Settings) makes one file and opens the share sheet. It goes only to the person or app you pick. It holds the app version, phone model, iOS version, time zone, your PitchClip settings, the team list and lineup with any names you typed, game and opponent names, the current pitch list, the details of clips from the last 3 days (no video), free storage space and recent log lines.

The developer uses what you send only to answer you and fix problems, keeps it only as long as that takes, never sells or shares it, and deletes it when you ask.

Purchases

PitchClip Premium is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account. PitchClip only asks the App Store whether Premium is active on this iPhone. The developer never receives your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers purchases.

Children

PitchClip is made for parents and coaches, not for children to use on their own. Because it films youth baseball, clips show children, and parents can type players' names and pick a player photo.

That information stays on the parent's iPhone. The developer does not collect, receive or store personal information about children through the app: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing goes to a server. A child's name or video reaches someone else only when a parent shares a clip, uses Live view, or sends feedback or a bug report.

PitchClip is intended for adults, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a child's information reaches the developer in feedback or a bug report, it is used only to fix the problem and is deleted on request. Parents can contact the developer at any time about their child's information.

Please think of other families when you share: clips can show other players, and Live view shows the lineup to nearby PitchClip phones.

Permissions

Camera and microphone: to film pitches with game sound, and for nothing else. Photos: to save clips you choose and, if you allow it, to read your newest screenshot for a lineup and to check that a moved clip arrived. Picking a player photo or one screenshot needs no Photos permission, because PitchClip sees only the picture you pick. The player photo is saved without its location or camera details. Local network: to find nearby phones for Live view. Notifications: alerts made on this phone, such as when recording stops. You can change each permission in the iPhone Settings app. PitchClip does not use your location.

Deleting your data

A deleted clip stays in Recently deleted for 7 days, then goes away on its own. To remove it sooner, open Settings, Storage and empty Recently deleted.

Settings, Delete all clips on this phone removes every clip and empties Recently deleted. Pitch counts, the team list, the player photo, settings and the log stay until you delete the app.

Remove the player photo in Settings, Player. Remove a player from your team list in Settings, My team, by swiping the player.

Deleting PitchClip removes everything it stored on this iPhone. Copies in an iPhone backup stay until that backup is replaced or deleted. Clips you saved to Photos or shared, and clips copied to other phones in Live view, stay where they are. To have the developer delete anything you emailed, write to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the web page for this policy, with a new effective date.
