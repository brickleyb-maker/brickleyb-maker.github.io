PitchClip

A fence camera for youth baseball parents. Clip your iPhone to the backstop fence, and PitchClip keeps a short clip of every pitch and counts them against Pitch Smart limits.

What it does

  • Records each pitch as its own clip, so there is nothing to trim later.
  • Counts our pitcher's pitches against Pitch Smart daily limits, rest days and tournament totals.
  • Estimates pitch speed from the video.
  • Tags clips by batter or pitcher, with a saved team roster.
  • Live view: other parents' phones nearby can watch the game and the count.
  • Game recaps and shareable speed cards.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your videos and your kids' names stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
